The whole editor, in your pocket.

Six tracks, titles in 66 fonts, colour and sound controls, and native export that keeps rendering while you use other apps. Free, with no account and nothing uploaded.

Download the APK

Android 7.0 or later. Development build; codec support varies by phone.

Install in a minute

  1. Download the APK on your phone and open it.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  3. Open Ordo, import video, photos or sound, and choose Add to sequence.
  4. Later updates install over the top. Ordo checks for a newer build when it starts and offers to download it.

Made for touch

  • Pinch to zoom the timeline, drag to scroll and drag clip edges to trim
  • Drag, resize and rotate titles right on the picture
  • Adjust colour and sound with a live preview, then compare with the original
  • Fullscreen preview that turns with your phone
