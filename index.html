Your media, the picture and the settings for the selected clip sit side by side above a timeline that shows every track. Tools open beside the picture, so you keep watching while you adjust.

  • Trim clip edges with the mouse, scrub along the ruler and zoom with Ctrl and the wheel
  • Drag files straight in from File Explorer
  • File, Edit and View menus, and shortcuts for everything you do often

Make anything move.

Add a keyframe, move the playhead, change the picture, and Ordo animates between them. Slow zooms, pans across a photo, a spinning overlay, music that fades under a voice: all set by hand, all free.

  • Keyframes for position, size, rotation, opacity and volume
  • Linear, ease in, ease out, ease in and out, or hold
  • Diamonds on the clip show every keyframe; tap one to jump to it
  • Splitting a clip keeps its motion smooth on both sides

See every clip at a glance.

Video clips show a strip of their pictures and sound clips show their waveform, so you can find the moment you want without playing it. Tools follow whatever you select, on your phone and on your PC.

  • Picture strips and waveforms drawn right inside the timeline
  • Right-click a clip, or long-press it on your phone, for Split, Duplicate, Adjust and Delete
  • Delete or split by accident? Undo is on the message that pops up
  • Imports start straight away, even with gigabytes of 4K footage

Titles that look designed.

Type straight onto the picture, then drag, resize and rotate it right there. Long lines wrap neatly between words.

  • 66 Google Fonts that ship inside the app and work offline
  • Bold, italic, outline, background, colour and opacity
  • Start and end animations from the same 13 effects as clips
  • Captions from SRT or VTT files, or written by hand

Colour and sound, shaped by hand.

Eleven picture controls cover exposure, contrast, highlights, shadows, temperature, tint, saturation, sharpening, vignette and fade. Flip the Adjusted switch to compare with the original.

  • Gain, pan, bass, mid and treble on every clip
  • A compressor and a limiter for even levels
  • Fades and equal-power crossfades between sound clips
  • Apply one look or one mix to all clips at once

Speed that still sounds right.

Slow a moment to 0.25× or rush through at 4×. Voices keep their natural pitch, and the clips after it slide along so nothing falls out of sync.

Exports that finish.

MP4 with H.264 or H.265, or WebM, from 480p to 4K at 24 to 60 fps. You see the estimated file size before you start, and on Android, native export keeps going while you switch apps.

The whole editor fits in your pocket.

On Android it is the same editor, laid out for your thumbs. Undo, redo and Export stay at the top. Tap a clip and the bar at the bottom changes to that clip's tools. Pinch to zoom, drag edges to trim and watch adjustments live.

Questions, answered.

Is Ordo really free?

Yes. Everything Ordo does today is free, with no account and no ads. Optional paid features may come later, but what is free now stays free. Report problems or ask for features on GitHub.

Do my videos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Ordo edits and exports on your device, and you choose which files it can open. The only connection it makes is the Android update check, which sends the app version and nothing about your media. The privacy statement has the details.

Can I move a project between my phone and my PC?

Projects are .ordo files that both apps open. Projects do not contain the media itself, so copy your original files across too and Ordo reconnects them by their content.

Can I keep using my phone while it exports?

Yes, when the export dialog shows native Android rendering. Allow notifications to see progress and cancel if needed, then return to Ordo to save. Some edits, such as colour grading and picture transitions, use the compatible exporter, which needs Ordo open.

Why no AI features?

Ordo is about hands-on editing. Everything it does to your picture and sound is a conventional tool you control, with no cloud processing.
